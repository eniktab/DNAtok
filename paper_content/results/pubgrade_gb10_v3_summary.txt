==== Key findings ====
* v3 wins the typical inference regimes (standard / short / large_batch)
  by 1.4-7.9x on all three BPE models.
* The "long" (B=8, T=8k) and "ultra_long" (B=2, T=32k) scenarios show
  v3 losing because the one-block-per-sequence design under-utilises
  the GPU when B is very small.
* Mitigation for production: when both B and T are small AND num_merges
  is large, route to HF. For everything else, use v3.
